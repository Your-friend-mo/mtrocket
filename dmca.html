DMCA & Copyright Compliance - MtRocket

MtRocket respects the intellectual property rights of creators and software authors. We maintain a strict policy of responding promptly to valid Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) notifications.

Notice and Takedown Procedure

If you are an authorized copyright holder or authorized representative and believe content hosted on MtRocket infringes your rights, submit a written notice to dmca@mtrocket.com including:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  • The specific URL link on MtRocket pointing to the allegedly infringing material.
  • Your contact information including legal name, email address, and phone number.
  • A statement of good faith belief that the disputed use is unauthorized.
  • A physical or electronic signature of the authorized copyright owner.

Upon receipt of a valid notice, our compliance department will expeditiously remove or disable access to the designated content within 24 hours.
