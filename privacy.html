Privacy Policy - MtRocket

Your privacy is of critical importance to MtRocket. This Privacy Policy outlines the minimal data collected, how it is safeguarded, and your complete rights as a visitor.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

MtRocket does not require registration, passwords, credit card details, or personal identifiable information (PII) to browse or download free mobile packages.

2. Technical Log Data

Like standard web platforms, our servers automatically record anonymous technical parameters such as IP address (truncated for geolocation routing), browser type, referring URLs, and operating system version solely to provide the correct download format for your device.

3. Cookies & Local Cache

We use lightweight local storage tokens strictly to preserve your user preferences (such as Light Mode or Dark Mode selection). We do not deploy cross-site tracking cookies.
